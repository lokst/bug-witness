Guest donor names are missing from the donation list

Donations made without an account still record the donor's name, and the
campaign page should display it. On the Help Build a Community Garden campaign
there is a $25 donation from "Guest Donor" with the message "Great cause!".

The message and the amount both appear, but the donor's name is blank. The
donation was not marked anonymous, so it should show the name that was given
with it rather than nothing at all.
